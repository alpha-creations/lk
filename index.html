About

The Mind Behind Alpha

Thushan Rathnayake

A 23-year-old Sri Lankan creative entrepreneur and student from Dambulla, who transforms bold ideas into immersive digital realities. With a relentless passion for futuristic design and modern technology, Thushan founded Alpha Creations to bridge the gap between imagination and innovation.

As a freelancer and multi-disciplinary creator, Thushan combines artistic vision with technical expertise — delivering everything from cinematic visuals to robust software solutions. Every project is a testament to the belief that creativity has no limits.

Alpha Creations isn't just an agency — it's a movement. A commitment to pushing digital boundaries and crafting experiences that leave lasting impressions.
